**Title:** Part-Time On-Call Dog Walker, Williamsburg, Brooklyn

**Location:** Williamsburg, Brooklyn, NY

**Pay:** [PAY]. On-call coverage pays a premium above our standard walking rate.

Not The Rug is a small, owner-run dog walking service in Williamsburg. We've been walking
the neighborhood's dogs since 2011.

We're building a dependable team of backup dog walkers. This is a part-time, on-call role
covering walks when a scheduled walker is unavailable or has an emergency. We're looking
for people who can step in within 24 hours of notification.

**How it works**

- You tell us which days and hours you can typically accept backup shifts.
- When a walk needs covering, we contact you by text, phone or email, whichever you prefer.
- We share the rate and coverage expectations before you accept work.
- Hours vary with coverage needs and are not guaranteed.
- You complete training and learn each dog's care instructions before covering walks on
  your own.

**You'll be a good fit if you**

- Can reliably get to Williamsburg, Brooklyn
- Have walked or cared for dogs, professionally, as a volunteer or personally
- Can respond to coverage requests during your available hours
- Are comfortable with extended walking, stairs and outdoor work in all weather, with or
  without reasonable accommodation
