Content Angle strategy recap

This post is doing two jobs at once. First, it establishes the "family" language and walker-relationship proof that Scout identified as the emotional core of your best reviews — without waiting for National Pet Day to justify it. Second, it positions Luis (or whichever named walker you feature in the photo) as a real person with real history in this neighborhood, so that when the April 11 post goes up, it reads as a continuation of something genuine rather than a holiday one-off. The behavior shift this targets is simple: a prospective client who has been casually considering dog walking should feel, after seeing this, that there is a real relationship available here — not just a booking slot. The soft CTA keeps the door open without pressure, which fits the trust-first posture the brand has earned since 2011.
