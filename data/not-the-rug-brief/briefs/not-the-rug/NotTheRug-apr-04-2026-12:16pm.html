Content Angle strategy recap

This post is the first beat of a two-part National Pet Day ramp. Posted today (April 4), it establishes NTR's trust positioning — consistent walker relationships, long-term local presence, individual attention — before the hashtag gets noisy on April 11. The strategy is to own the emotional territory first, so the day-of post can be shorter and warmer rather than having to do all the credibility work in one caption. The perception shift this is trying to create: NTR isn't a service you book, it's a relationship your dog already has. That framing quietly separates the brand from platform competitors without naming them. The soft mention of April 11 gives the post a reason to exist today without making a future event the lead — it reads as context, not a countdown.
