National Pet Day is April 11. We're getting ready for it the same way we do everything — with an actual dog and an actual person behind the leash.

Luis has been walking dogs in Williamsburg since 2011. His take on pack walking: "When you're moving six dogs at once, you're managing a group. When you're with one or two dogs you know, you're actually with them."

That's the whole thing, really.

If you've got a dog who deserves that kind of attention — or you've been meaning to reach out — DM us ahead of the weekend.

Content Angle strategy recap

This post is a scheduled asset for April 11 but written now so the founder has time to pair it with a real photo of a client dog before the date hits. The strategic move is using National Pet Day not as a generic holiday peg but as a moment to let Luis speak in his own voice about what separates a real walker relationship from volume-based services. The quote does the differentiation work without naming competitors or making it a comparison post. The emotion this is designed to trigger is quiet trust — the kind that makes a dog owner think "that's the person I want" rather than "that's a good deal." Recommend sourcing one clean, natural photo of a familiar client dog on route in the neighborhood to pair with this caption. If a Story sequence is possible, a second frame with the quote pulled out as text on a simple background would extend the shelf life through April 11.

⚠ The phrase 'If you've got a dog who deserves that kind of attention' uses slightly patronizing framing ('deserves') that edges toward the avoid category of 'overexplaining obvious dog-owner basics' and subtle guilt-touch.
